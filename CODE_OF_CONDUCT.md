# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Accept feedback gracefully, protect other people's privacy and avoid publishing confidential configuration or copyrighted content.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the repository owner through the contact method configured on GitHub. The owner may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be fair, proportionate and documented privately.

This project will adopt a full community standard, such as Contributor Covenant, before accepting substantial external contributions.
